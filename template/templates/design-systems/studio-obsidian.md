# Design System Preset: Warm Obsidian & Brass (Studio Craft Style)

A tactile, high-contrast dark studio design system combining warm obsidian surfaces, brushed gold/brass accents, editorial serif display titles, and spatial cover-flow interaction.

## 0. UI Design Dials
- **`DESIGN_VARIANCE`**: `8` (Spatial cover-flow stages, warm studio craft, bespoke editorial hierarchy)
- **`MOTION_INTENSITY`**: `7` (Fluid 3D cover-flow physics, tactile hover lifts, smooth drawer transitions)
- **`VISUAL_DENSITY`**: `6` (Balanced spatial breathing room with focused action cards and monospace receipts)

## 1. Color Palette & Semantic Tokens
- **Background (Canvas)**: `#0C0C0E` (Deep warm obsidian)
- **Surface / Card**: `#141418` (Elevated studio slate)
- **Primary / Accent**: `#D4AF37` (Warm studio gold / brass)
- **Primary Hover**: `#E5C158` (Bright burnished gold)
- **Text Primary**: `#F4F4F6` (Crisp warm parchment white)
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: `#9CA3AF` (Muted stone gray)
- **Border / Divider**: `#232329` (`1px solid` subtle dark border)
- **Error / Danger**: `#EF4444`
- **Warning**: `#F59E0B`
- **Success**: `#10B981`

## 2. Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Font**: `'Instrument Serif'`, `'Playfair Display'`, `Georgia`, `serif`
- **UI / Body Font**: `'Inter'`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- **Monospace Font**: `'JetBrains Mono'`, `'Fira Code'`, `monospace`

## 3. Spacing & Elevation
- **Base Grid**: `8px` increments
- **Elevation**: Subtle `1px solid #232329` borders with warm radial ambient glows on active cards
- **Corner Radius**: `12px` for cards/drawers, `8px` for controls and pills
